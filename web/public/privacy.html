BudgetMate Privacy Policy

Effective August 2, 2026

This policy describes how the BudgetMate beta handles information in its iOS and web apps.

Information BudgetMate handles

BudgetMate processes the account, profile, household, budget, transaction, split, and settlement information you choose to enter. Account authentication can include an email address and a service-generated user identifier.

Local and cloud storage

The iOS app stores budget data on your device. The web app can store data in your browser. When you sign in or use a shared budget, the information required for that feature is sent to the configured Supabase service so authorized household members can access it.

Service providers

BudgetMate uses Supabase for authentication and optional cloud synchronization. The BudgetMate web app is delivered through Cloudflare Pages. Those services process technical and account information needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

Support archives

The iOS app can create a support archive when you request one. An archive can contain budget information. Review it before sharing and do not post it, financial details, passwords, or access tokens in a public support request.

Beta controls

Some account and shared-household lifecycle controls are still unavailable in the beta. BudgetMate shows those unavailable actions in the app instead of silently deleting or changing shared financial history.

Questions

For general questions, use the BudgetMate support page. The support channel is public, so do not include personal or financial information.
