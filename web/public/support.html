BudgetMate Support

BudgetMate is currently a controlled beta. For a reproducible bug or general product question, open an issue in the public BudgetMate repository.

Helpful details

Include your device model, iOS or browser version, the screen involved, and the steps that caused the problem. Screenshots are useful only after you remove personal and financial information.

Do not post account emails, passwords, access tokens, transaction details, amounts, household identifiers, database output, or support archives. GitHub issues are public.
